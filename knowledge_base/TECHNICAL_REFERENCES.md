# Authoring references and fictional scope

All NovaFiber commercial terms, plans, fees, credits, dates, operating targets, and device models are invented for this dataset. The cited vendors do not endorse NovaFiber and their manuals do not define NF-ONT-100 or NF-RTR-200 LED meanings. Articles are written in original wording rather than copied from provider policies.

Generic optical safety principles were checked against [Cisco laser safety guidance](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/td/docs/routers/nfvis/c8300-ucpe/c8300-ucpe-hig/laser-safety-guidelines.html): unterminated optical connectors can emit invisible radiation and must not be viewed directly. NovaFiber's customer policy deliberately reserves all connector handling to authorized technicians; Cisco's professional inspection instructions are not customer troubleshooting steps.

Generic band/range trade-offs were checked against [TP-Link's 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz explanation](https://www.tp-link.com/ca/support/faq/499/) and [TP-Link Wi-Fi placement/range guidance](https://www.tp-link.com/au/support/faq/468/). NF-EQ-003 uses only basic qualitative concepts, without copying device-specific advanced settings or promising coverage.

These references are authoring context, not additional upload files. Do not ingest this document into the customer policy collection. When adapting the corpus to real equipment, validate it against the exact manufacturer's manual and local installation requirements before operational use.
