## 1. Qualify the address and place an order
Use the verified account portal or the official support contact in your service paperwork to request an address check. NovaFiber assesses the building route, unit access, and permission for installation before confirming serviceability. A neighbor's connection is not proof that another unit is eligible. Nova cannot check live availability, submit an order, or collect personal documents. This fictional corpus intentionally includes no live coverage list, invented phone number, or public signup URL.

## 2. Select a plan and confirm the quote
Essential 100 is $39/month, Everyday 300 $59/month, Home 600 $79/month, and Gig 1000 $99/month, in USD before applicable itemized taxes. All four have up to symmetric fiber rates in Mbps, no residential data cap, and month-to-month service without a standard early termination charge. Standard installation costs $49 once and includes a loaned ONT and router with no monthly rental. Nonstandard work requires a separately accepted itemized quote. Subscription billing begins only when service is activated, not when the order is submitted.

## 3. Prepare for the installation visit
After site qualification and required permissions, the usual planning target is an appointment within three to five business days, subject to available capacity; it is not a guaranteed repair or installation SLA. Appointment windows are 9 am-1 pm or 1 pm-5 pm in the service address's local time. A responsible adult aged 18 or older must be present, with landlord/building permission arranged if required. Clear safe access to the agreed equipment location and a suitable electrical outlet. The technician, not the customer, handles optical connectors.

## 4. Reschedule or report a missed appointment
Give at least 24 hours' notice through verified support to reschedule without a missed-appointment charge. Customer absence, denied access, or a later change may incur $25 after review and another slot must be arranged. Do not assume every late arrival or disputed visit automatically triggers a fee. If NovaFiber cancels or cannot attend, there is no customer missed-visit fee; support offers another slot. Nova cannot view availability, book a visit, or confirm a technician's arrival time.

## 5. Accept and check completed service
The technician installs and activates the ONT/router, checks optical registration, and demonstrates an appropriate wired connectivity test and basic Wi-Fi access. Record the device models and customer handover instructions; keep credential labels private. Wi-Fi coverage and throughput depend on placement and devices, and plan rates are not a promise of speed in every room. Confirm service can load websites before treating the installation as working. Follow NF-EQ-002 for approved customer configuration and NF-EQ-003 for band/coverage guidance.

## 6. Failed qualification and cancellation before activation
If the address cannot be qualified, collected base subscription and standard installation charges are refundable within 10 business days after cancellation confirmation. A customer cancellation before activation when no installation work has begun has the same standard-charge refund timing. Separately accepted nonstandard work already completed is governed by its quote. NF-BP-003 controls refunds and equipment obligations after activation. Nova can explain these policies but cannot determine whether work began, approve an exception, or confirm that payment was refunded.
