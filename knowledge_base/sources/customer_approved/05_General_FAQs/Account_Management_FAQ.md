## 1. Verify an account change safely
The account holder uses the authenticated portal or a verified support channel to change contact details, billing preferences, or service. Support verifies authority before acting. Never send passwords, one-time codes, full payment-card data, or identity-document images to Nova. The current chatbot explains policy only and cannot sign in, update an account, or verify that the person asking owns it. A request made in chat is not an authenticated service order.

## 2. Upgrade a residential plan
Choose from Essential 100 ($39), Everyday 300 ($59), Home 600 ($79), or Gig 1000 ($99) monthly base charges in USD. An upgrade depends on address and equipment compatibility, and takes effect after verified confirmation, normally within one business day. The additional base charge is prorated for the remaining calendar days in the cycle, including the effective change day. Example: upgrading Everyday 300 to Home 600 with 15 days remaining in a 30-day cycle adds ($79-$59) x 15/30 = $10. Applicable invoice tax adjustments are separate. There is no standard plan-change administration fee.

## 3. Downgrades and contact updates
Downgrades take effect at the next billing-cycle start; they do not create a mid-cycle downgrade refund. Changes to email, phone, billing address, or AutoPay settings require account-holder verification and confirmation through the portal or support. Changing a billing address does not relocate an installed fiber service. Updating a Wi-Fi password is a local equipment setting under NF-EQ-002, not an account password change. Nova cannot choose an effective date on a customer's behalf.

## 4. Lost credentials and access questions
Use the portal's verified recovery process or the support contact printed in the service agreement. Do not use a phone number or link invented by a chatbot. NovaFiber credentials are not included in this corpus. A tenant, family member, or property owner is not automatically authorized to change another person's account. Requests involving ownership transfer, deceased account holders, or identity disputes need verified Customer Care review; this corpus does not establish document requirements or promise approval for those cases.

## 5. Supplied router or ONT replacement
Report the model, symptoms, indicator states, and safe checks completed. NovaFiber diagnoses whether a supplied device needs replacement before arranging it. Standard replacement for a confirmed equipment fault not caused by the customer has no equipment fee. Misuse damage or missing equipment can result in reviewed charges of $80 for the router or $120 for the ONT, with the reason itemized. Ordinary wear is not misuse. Never open an ONT or remove optical connectors to return it yourself. Exact replacement timing depends on diagnosis, stock, and appointment availability.

## 6. Other requests and actual account status
Cancellation/refunds use NF-BP-003, relocation uses NF-GF-002, and invoice disputes use NF-BP-002. These policies do not establish an actual account balance, successful plan upgrade, replacement approval, or service appointment. Nova may explain what to request and which information is safe to prepare; it cannot perform any of these account actions or offer unpublished discounts.
