## 1. Check the new address before planning a move
Ask verified NovaFiber support to qualify the full new service address, including unit and building access. Coverage elsewhere in the neighborhood does not guarantee service in a particular building. Nova has no live coverage database and cannot confirm a city, street, or unit is serviceable. This corpus intentionally publishes no coverage map or real office address. Existing-account verification is required before an actual relocation order.

## 2. Request timing and standard charge
Submit a relocation request at least seven calendar days before the desired move when possible. This is planning guidance, not a guaranteed installation window. A standard relocation to a qualified address costs $49 once, separate from the continuing monthly plan charge. Nonstandard installation work requires a separately accepted quote. NovaFiber does not charge both a standard relocation fee and a second standard new-installation fee for the same approved relocation. Appointments depend on site permission and capacity.

## 3. Handle equipment and optical connections safely
Do not pull, cut, unplug, or transport the installed optical termination yourself. Leave the ONT connected until support gives safe technician-assisted removal instructions. The customer may transport the loaned router and its adapter when instructed. The existing ONT may remain or be replaced depending on the installation design; support confirms the equipment plan. Keep supplied equipment safe and follow NF-EQ-004. Do not move service to a new address merely by plugging a router into another provider's wall port.

## 4. Coordinate service dates and billing
Support confirms the old-address disconnection date and new-address activation date in writing. Monthly base charges continue through the confirmed old-address service date and resume at the new activation date. A provider-approved gap between those dates is excluded from base-subscription billing, with calendar-day proration applied to the affected cycle. This relocation gap is not also credited as a network outage for the same period. Customer-requested overlap requires explicit agreement; this residential corpus does not promise two active connections for one base charge.

## 5. If the new address is not covered
NovaFiber cannot complete relocation to an unqualified address. The account holder may keep existing service until a chosen verified cancellation date or cancel under NF-BP-003. There is no standard early termination fee. Unused prepaid base subscription after cancellation is refunded according to that policy; completed old-address installation and accepted completed work are not automatically refunded. A relocation fee collected for work not begun at an unavailable address is refunded within 10 business days after cancellation confirmation. Support confirms equipment return instructions.

## 6. Appointments and completion
Before the visit, secure building/landlord permission where needed and arrange a responsible adult at the address. Give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule; a missed appointment or later change may incur a $25 charge after review. Provider-cancelled visits have no missed-visit fee. Do not promise a specific technician or completion date without operational confirmation. After activation, test a wired and Wi-Fi device and retain the relocation confirmation for billing questions.
