## 1. Current plan catalog
All listed prices are invented USD monthly base charges. Essential 100 offers up to 100 Mbps download and 100 Mbps upload for $39/month. Everyday 300 offers up to 300 Mbps download and 300 Mbps upload for $59/month. Home 600 offers up to 600 Mbps download and 600 Mbps upload for $79/month. Gig 1000 offers up to 1000 Mbps download and 1000 Mbps upload for $99/month. These are residential fiber plans, subject to address qualification. No promotional discount, mobile bundle, television package, or fixed-term contract is included in this catalog.

## 2. Charges and equipment included
Standard installation is a one-time $49 charge for an eligible existing residential fiber route and ordinary indoor termination. Any nonstandard work requires a separate itemized quote accepted before work begins. One NovaFiber ONT and one Wi-Fi router are loaned without monthly rental; ownership remains with NovaFiber. Government-imposed taxes, if applicable, appear separately on the invoice; the corpus does not define a local tax rate. There is no additional standard activation fee, residential data cap, or standard early termination fee.

## 3. Speed and service qualifications
Plan rates are up to Mbps measured at the service connection; Wi-Fi performance varies with radio conditions and device capability. A 1 Gbps Ethernet link can yield about 940 Mbps of application throughput because of overhead. NovaFiber does not promise identical wired and wireless results or a contractual 70% minimum. The 70% threshold in NF-TS-003 is only a diagnostic trigger. Plans do not include a dedicated business circuit, guaranteed uptime, managed security, or a public static IP address.

## 4. Billing and fair service use
Subscriptions are month-to-month and billed in advance from the service activation date. The billing cycle follows that activation-day anniversary, using the last day of a month when the anniversary day does not exist. The invoice is due seven calendar days after issue. There is no prepaid recharge expiry process; customers pay invoices, not arbitrary day-based recharge packs. Lawful household use has no volume cap. Network abuse or a security incident may require a separate investigation under NF-BP-004; heavy lawful use alone is not grounds for an invented data-cap charge.

## 5. Plan changes
An authenticated account holder may request a plan change through the account portal or verified support. Upgrades, if supported at the address, take effect after confirmation, normally within one business day, with a prorated difference for the remaining cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next billing-cycle start with no mid-cycle downgrade refund. No standard plan-change administration fee applies. See NF-GF-001 for verification and examples. Nova cannot alter a plan or confirm a customer's current subscription.

## 6. Other policies and exceptions
Use NF-BP-002 for payment processing and disputes, NF-BP-003 for cancellation/refunds/equipment returns, NF-GF-002 for relocation, and NF-GF-003 for service qualification and first installation. Final qualification, tax treatment, any nonstandard work, and appointment availability must be confirmed for the specific address. Do not fabricate coverage, a discounted price, or a service guarantee from this general catalog.
