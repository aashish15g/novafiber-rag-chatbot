## 1. Nonpayment timeline
Monthly invoices are due seven calendar days after issue. For an unpaid undisputed amount, NovaFiber sends reminders and a suspension notice at least three calendar days before suspension. Suspension may begin when that amount is 10 calendar days past its due date. Example: an invoice issued October 1 is due October 8; suspension may begin October 18 if the required notice was sent by October 15. These dates are a fictional policy example, not a statement about a particular customer's account.

## 2. Disputes and pending payments
A specific amount formally accepted as disputed by billing support is held from nonpayment suspension during review. Undisputed amounts remain payable on schedule. A chatbot conversation does not establish a formal dispute or hold. If payment was successfully debited but not matched, contact billing with the transaction reference rather than paying twice. Bank transfers may take two business days to post. Verified support must review the evidence and any suspension risk; Nova cannot confirm a pending transfer or grant an extension.

## 3. What suspension means
Nonpayment suspension restricts service and does not cancel the account. Base subscription billing continues until verified cancellation under NF-BP-003. There is no standard late fee or reconnection fee in this fictional residential policy; outstanding invoice amounts remain due. Do not assume a suspension solely from an amber router Internet light. An equipment fault or outage can produce a similar symptom. Current suspension status requires authenticated account information.

## 4. Reconnection after settled payment
Once all overdue undisputed amounts are posted to the account, normal nonpayment reconnection is targeted within four hours. This is a processing target, not a guarantee or evidence that a specific account has been restored. A bank transfer initiation is not the same as a posted payment. If service remains unavailable after four hours from confirmed posting, contact verified support with the payment reference. Follow safe diagnostics in NF-TS-001 only when the optical indicators are normal; repeated resets do not settle an invoice.

## 5. Security and misuse cases
NovaFiber may temporarily restrict a connection during a verified network-security or unlawful-use investigation, distinct from routine nonpayment. Support communicates available information and required remediation through a verified channel when permissible. The nonpayment four-hour reconnection target does not apply automatically to a security restriction. The chatbot must not accuse a customer of misuse, invent a violation, disclose internal detection rules, or recommend bypassing a restriction. There is no residential data-volume cap that triggers suspension for ordinary heavy lawful use.

## 6. Customer options and limits
The authenticated account holder may review invoices, settle confirmed amounts, request a formal billing dispute, ask support about circumstances, or submit cancellation under NF-BP-003. Any case-specific arrangement requires written confirmation from support and is not promised in this article. Nova explains the process but cannot inspect balances, remove a restriction, extend a due date, or promise immediate restoration. Current outages are a separate operational question and cannot be inferred from an unpaid-bill policy.
