## 1. Invoice timing and payment methods
NovaFiber bills subscriptions in advance on the activation-day anniversary; a missing anniversary day uses that month's final day. Payment is due seven calendar days after invoice issue. The authenticated account portal accepts debit/credit cards and bank transfer. AutoPay is optional and, when authorized by the account holder, attempts payment on the due date. Card payments normally post immediately, while bank transfers may take up to two business days after successful initiation. Business days mean Monday through Friday excluding holidays observed by NovaFiber support; exact holidays require support confirmation.

## 2. Read the first invoice
The first invoice normally contains the chosen monthly base charge plus the $49 standard installation fee and applicable itemized taxes. Service activation, not order submission or an uncompleted visit, starts subscription billing. Nonstandard work appears only after the customer accepts a separate quote. Plans include one loaned ONT/router without monthly rental. Nova cannot retrieve an actual invoice or decide whether a specific tax is correct; the account holder must use the authenticated portal or verified billing support.

## 3. Payment debited but invoice still unpaid
Keep the bank or payment receipt and transaction reference. Do not pay again simply because a pending transfer has not posted. Allow up to two business days for bank transfer posting; for a successfully debited card that remains unmatched, contact billing support instead of repeatedly retrying. Share only the reference, payment date, amount, and relevant invoice number through a verified support channel. Never provide a full card number, CVV, bank password, or one-time code to Nova or in a ticket's free-text description.

## 4. Failed attempts and duplicate payments
For an explicitly failed payment with no debit, verify the payment details privately and use a supported method. A failed attempt does not extend the invoice due date. If two successful payments settle against the same invoice, billing reviews the receipts and refunds a verified duplicate to the original method within five business days after approval. A pending authorization is not necessarily a settled duplicate. The chatbot cannot see settlement status or approve a refund; do not promise the bank's posting time.

## 5. Billing dispute procedure
Raise an invoice dispute within 30 calendar days of its issue date, identifying the charge and reason. Billing acknowledges the case within two business days and aims to provide a decision within five business days after receiving the necessary evidence; unusual cases require an update. Once support accepts a specific charge as formally disputed, that amount is held from nonpayment suspension while reviewed. Undisputed amounts remain due. Merely telling Nova about a dispute does not open a case or create a hold. See NF-BP-004 for suspension timing.

## 6. Refunds, cancellation, and status checks
Approved duplicate-payment refunds use the timing above. Unused subscription refunds after cancellation and verified outage credits use NF-BP-003, which defines their separate eligibility rules. Check actual balance, AutoPay authorization, payment matching, and dispute status in authenticated systems. Static documents establish procedures, not the current balance or whether a named customer's payment was received. Nova may explain charges and route a customer to verified support, but cannot collect payment or modify an account.
