## 1. Submit an authenticated cancellation request
The account holder may request cancellation through the authenticated portal or verified support, stating the account and requested date. Cancellation takes effect on the requested date or, if that date has passed, on the date the verified request is received. Support confirms the effective date in writing. There is no standard early termination charge for these month-to-month plans. Asking Nova to cancel does not constitute a verified request or stop billing. Billing continues through the confirmed cancellation date, inclusive.

## 2. Calculate unused subscription refunds
After confirmed cancellation, prepaid base subscription charges for calendar days after the effective cancellation date are refundable. Divide that cycle's base subscription charge by the number of calendar days in that billing cycle, multiply by the unused days after cancellation, then round once to the nearest cent. Example: on a $59 plan in a 30-day cycle, 10 unused days yield $19.67. Reconcile outstanding valid charges before refunding any net balance. Completed installation and completed accepted nonstandard work are not refundable merely because the customer cancels. Tax adjustments follow the final invoice; this corpus defines no local tax treatment.

## 3. Return loaned equipment
Return the NovaFiber router, ONT, and supplied adapters within 14 calendar days after the confirmed cancellation date, using the return instructions supplied by support. Customers must not disconnect the ONT's optical connector; support arranges safe removal where necessary and the deadline is extended until that provider-controlled removal is completed. Keep proof of return. Missing or customer-damaged loaned equipment may result in an itemized $80 router charge and/or $120 ONT charge after review. Normal wear and diagnosed non-customer-caused failure are not charged. A return charge is conditional, not an automatic cancellation fee.

## 4. Verified outage credit eligibility
A continuous loss of NovaFiber internet caused by a provider-network fault is eligible for a prorated base-subscription credit when the verified outage lasts at least four hours. Support verifies the affected service, start/end times, and cause. Customer power loss, local Wi-Fi coverage problems, customer-caused damage, and nonpayment suspension are excluded. Compute credit as cycle base subscription divided by the cycle's calendar days, multiplied by verified eligible outage hours divided by 24; round once to cents. Example: $59 in a 30-day cycle with a verified 12-hour outage yields $0.98. Credits cannot exceed the base subscription charge for that cycle and do not overlap a refund for the same unused service period.

## 5. Request and settlement timing
Request an outage credit within 30 calendar days after service restoration, supplying the relevant ticket/reference through verified support. Approved outage credits appear on the next invoice, or in the final balance reconciliation if the account is closing. Approved net cancellation refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days after final-balance and equipment-return reconciliation. Verified duplicate-payment refunds instead use the five-business-day rule in NF-BP-002. Provider issuance is distinct from the payment institution's final posting time.

## 6. Orders before activation and policy limits
If NovaFiber cannot qualify the address before activation, collected base subscription and standard installation charges are refunded within 10 business days after cancellation confirmation. If the customer cancels before activation and no installation work has begun, those collected standard charges are also refundable on that timing. A separately accepted nonstandard-work quote governs work already completed under that quote. Nova cannot verify outage duration, calculate an actual final balance, apply a credit, authorize an exception, or confirm that equipment was returned.
