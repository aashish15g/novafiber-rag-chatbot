## 1. Internal scope and public policy alignment
Do not ingest this article into the customer collection. Customer-approved appointment information is in NF-GF-003 and NF-GF-002. Standard new installation and standard relocation each cost $49 in their respective contexts; do not charge both for one approved relocation. A diagnosed provider network/equipment fault not caused by the customer has no standard repair visit charge. Any chargeable nonstandard work needs an itemized customer-accepted quote before execution; this runbook does not authorize hidden charges.

## 2. Dispatch criteria after remote diagnosis
Consider a visit for persistent optical faults with no confirmed shared incident, visible site cable damage, an approved equipment replacement needing safe optical removal, or an installation/relocation requiring physical work. Record indicator states, remote diagnostic results when available, and why remote steps are insufficient. Red LOS alone does not identify a specific failed component. For a confirmed area incident, link affected tickets and prioritize network restoration; avoid dispatching duplicate household visits without an independent site reason.

## 3. Prepare the appointment safely
Verify account authority, service address, access permission, responsible adult availability, and any reported electrical or site hazard. Staff must not instruct customers to inspect or clean fiber connectors. A hazardous site may require qualified remediation before broadband work. Choose an actually available 9 am-1 pm or 1 pm-5 pm local-time slot in the scheduling system; the existence of those windows does not establish availability. Confirm the date/window in writing after a successful reservation. Technician identities and schedules remain operational data.

## 4. Rescheduling and missed visits
Customer changes at least 24 hours before the slot start have no missed-appointment charge. Customer absence, access refusal, or a later change may incur $25 after review, with the reason recorded and communicated. If NovaFiber cancels or cannot attend, do not charge the customer; offer another actual slot. Do not bill automatically based only on a technician's unverified note. Check disputed access/arrival records before deciding. Weather, emergency hazards, and provider delays require a new communicated plan rather than an unsupported promise of same-day arrival.

## 5. Field evidence and equipment handling
The authorized technician documents the relevant optical/physical checks, repairs, equipment model/serial references in the protected system, and service verification. Do not publish real identifiers in this knowledge base. For cancellation returns, customers have 14 calendar days after cancellation, extended when safe ONT removal depends on NovaFiber's visit. Record provider-controlled removal completion before applying a non-return deadline. Reviewed missing/customer-damaged equipment charges are $80 router and $120 ONT; normal wear or non-customer-caused failure is excluded under NF-BP-003.

## 6. Closure and follow-up
After work, validate optical registration and browsing, and record an appropriate wired test when relevant. Explain what changed, any remaining limitation, and the follow-up contact. Do not convert Wi-Fi coverage concerns into a guaranteed speed in every room. Update the ticket with verified work and customer communication; a failed visit remains open with an owner and next action. The customer chatbot cannot use this article to book dispatch, disclose private field records, or assert a technician is on the way.
